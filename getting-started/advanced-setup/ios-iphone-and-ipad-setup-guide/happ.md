## Step 1: Download Happ for iOS

[Click here](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oneclick-safe-easy-fast/id1545555197) to be redirected to the app page in the iOS App Store.

{% hint style="success" %}
After successfully installing the app, proceed below to setup
{% endhint %}

***

## Step 2: Import the servers

### Manual Setup

1. [Click here to login to your dashboard](https://my.coneaccount.com/)
2. Scroll down to Quick Import section and tap on Copy Link

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/8b23a74bb4d42e10ed15d01f156325eb.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Paste the api link into the app

1. Open the Happ app.
2. Click on the "+" icon on the top right corner.
3. Tap on **Subscription URL**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (112).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Paste the link into the **URL** box, then tap on **Done**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (113).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## **Step 3: Connect**

1. Tap on the **Connect** button
2. First time will ask you to **Add VPN Configuratio**. Click Allow and enter your pin/touch ID/face ID.
3. Your device will be connected.
