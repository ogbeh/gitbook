# Guide: Switch Apple account

{% hint style="info" %}
Once the app has been installed successfully, you can switch back to your personal Apple ID normally.
{% endhint %}

## Sign out of your current account

1. Open the App Store on your iOS device
2. Tap on the profile icon

<div align="center"><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/1.png" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap on **Sign Out**
4. Scroll to the top of the page, then tap on **Sign in With Apple Account**
5. When you see the prompt:

> Set up Media & Purchases as (your account name)

Tap "**Not (your account name)"**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (114).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. User our Apple account login details to login

#### [Tap here to get our Apple account details](https://app.alekwu.top/soft/shrkios.html)

7. Paste the email and password and tap on **Continue**
8. On the **Apple Account Security** page, tap on **Other Options**
9. Tap on **DO NOT UPGRADE**

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (105).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="success" %}
✅ You should be signed in successfully.
{% endhint %}

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 🔐 Is It Safe to Use Our Apple ID?

> ✅ **Yes — 100% safe.**\
> You are **only signing into the App Store**, not your whole iPhone.

* Your contacts, photos, and messages **will not be affected**
* Your iCloud or device settings **stay untouched**
* After download, it will **automatically switch back** to your original Apple ID
* You can still log back into your own Apple ID any time

**💡 You're only using our Apple ID temporarily to download Shadowrocket. That’s it.**
